Selected work

Autonomous frontier exploration

Working

A mobile robot that maps an unknown environment on its own. It runs SLAM while a custom frontier-search node finds the boundary between mapped and unmapped space, ranks candidate frontiers, and hands goals to the navigation stack until there is nothing left to explore.

The frontier node is written from scratch rather than using explore_lite, which meant owning the detection, clustering, and goal-selection logic end to end.

ROS 2 · slam_toolbox · Nav2 · C++ / Python · Gazebo

Vision-guided grasping on a UR3

In progress

Perception-to-grasp pipeline in simulation: YOLOv8 detects objects in the camera frame, detections are projected into the robot's workspace, and the arm plans and executes a pick. Builds on hands-on work with UR3 forward and inverse kinematics.

YOLOv8 · ROS 2 · Gazebo · UR3 · OpenCV

Compliant gripper

In progress

Designing and building a gripper mechanism, then running finite element analysis on the grasp forces to make the fingers compliant — letting the structure absorb positioning error instead of demanding perfect alignment from the controller.

Fusion 360 · FEA · 3D printing · tolerance fitting

Conglobation mechanics in pill bugs

Research

Bio-inspired research into how pill bugs roll into a sealed sphere. Working from micro-CT scans to reconstruct the segment geometry, modeling the articulation, and running FEA to understand how the shell distributes load when closed — with an eye toward armored mechanisms that fold.

Micro-CT · Fusion 360 · MATLAB · FEA
