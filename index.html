01 — MECHANICAL DESIGN

Mechanical Gripper

47.5% mass reduction17.7 N grip force4.86× factor of safety

A 3D-printed, nine-part parallel-jaw gripper producing 17.7 N of grip force from a 6V servo through a three-gear, 1.5:1 reduction. A six-point parametric FEA study on jaw thickness and geometry cut mass by 47.5% while holding a 4.86× factor of safety, backed by a regression model predicting stress and displacement under load.

Fusion 360 · FEA · 3D printing · GD&T · ASME Y14.5

02 — ROBOTICS & AUTONOMY

Autonomous Robot Exploration

10/10 completed trials2.9 min average run

An autonomous exploration pipeline for a TurtleBot3 in ROS 2/Gazebo, pairing slam_toolbox for real-time SLAM with Nav2 and a custom BFS-based frontier detector. 100% completion across 10 randomized-start trials, averaging 2.9 minutes per run with progress-based frontier blacklisting to prevent navigation loops.

ROS 2 · slam_toolbox · Nav2 · C++ / Python · Gazebo

03 — PERCEPTION & CONTROL

UR3 Robotic Arm Drawing Pipeline

6 DOF inverse kinematics17% less travel

A closed-form 6-DOF inverse-kinematics solver for a UR3, converting target end-effector poses into joint commands on physical hardware. A ROS/Python perception-to-actuation pipeline — blue-channel segmentation, Canny edge detection, contour extraction — autonomously draws test images, with nearest-neighbor contour sequencing cutting unnecessary travel by 17%.

UR3 · OpenCV · Python · forward / inverse kinematics
